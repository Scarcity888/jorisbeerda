Speaker bio

Joris Beerda is CEO of The Octalysis Group and the world's leading practitioner of Octalysis. Endorsed by Yu-kai Chou as the #1 Octalysis gamification designer, he designs engagement systems for Fortune 500 companies, governments, and high-growth startups.

A former diplomat who applied behavioral science in conflict zones, Joris now helps audiences see why most gamification stalls at badges, and how the eight Core Drives of human motivation can shift conversion, loyalty, learning, and habit formation at scale. His work has reached 170+ enterprise clients across 30 countries and impacted 1.5 billion users.

Signature talks

Topics adapted from real enterprise work.

01

Beyond Badges: Enterprise Gamification That Moves Metrics

Why points and leaderboards fail, and how Octalysis-driven design ties motivation to revenue, retention, adherence, and daily active usage.

02

The Octalysis Framework: Eight Core Drives of Motivation

A practical tour of Yu-kai Chou's framework, from Epic Meaning to Scarcity, with examples of how each Core Drive shows up in products people actually use.

03

Conversion Science: Closing the Intention-Action Gap

Scarcity, urgency, and social influence as architecture, not gimmicks. Lessons from conversion work at global scale for digital marketplaces.

04

Loyalty Beyond Points: Lifestyle Engagement

How to turn transactional rewards into daily habits. Designing loyalty ecosystems that reward behavior, exploration, and empowerment, not only spend.

05

Behavior Change at Scale: Sales Forces, Learning, and Health Habits

Applying Core Drives to sales enablement, technical learning platforms, and patient adherence. How narrative, social relatedness, and progression trees shift real outcomes.
